Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Overview

ARKA ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our personal drive assistant application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you access or use ARKA.

2. Google User Data & Drive Integration

ARKA integrates with Google APIs using the drive.file scope:

  • Google Authentication: We access your basic profile information (name, email address, avatar) solely to authenticate and identify your personal session.
  • Google Drive BYOD Storage: When granted access, ARKA uploads and manages files solely within a dedicated folder (e.g. "ARKA Files") in your personal Google Drive account.
  • Data Ownership: You retain 100% ownership of all files stored in your Google Drive. ARKA does not sell, transfer, or disclose your Google Drive files or metadata to third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Security

All communication with Google APIs and ARKA services occurs exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections. Authentication tokens are handled securely and never exposed publicly.

4. Revoking Access

You can disconnect ARKA from your Google Account at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.
